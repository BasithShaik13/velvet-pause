About us

Where charging becomes hospitality

The Velvet Pause is a premium EV charging hub that transforms ordinary charging stops into comfortable, convenient and enjoyable experiences, combining wireless charging technology with lounges, dining, shopping and workspaces.

Our Mission

To transform EV charging into a comfortable, safe, and premium experience through innovative wireless charging technology and customer-focused amenities.

Our Vision

To become a trusted destination for premium EV charging and hospitality experiences.

Our Purpose

To make EV charging safer, more convenient and more enjoyable while encouraging sustainable transportation.
